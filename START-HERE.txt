TURMOIL RESOURCE TRACKER - WINDOWS DEMO

Node.js 22 or newer must already be installed.

1. Right-click the downloaded ZIP file and choose "Extract All".
2. Open the extracted folders until you see START-TURMOIL.bat.
3. Double-click START-TURMOIL.bat.

The first launch installs the app's required files. This needs an internet
connection and may take a few minutes. After that, your browser opens the app.

Keep the black launcher window open while using the tracker.
To use it again later, double-click START-TURMOIL.bat again.

This is a demo with sample clan members. You do not need to configure Discord
or a database to try it. Demo changes are saved on this computer.

To give your real clan access, follow the Discord and Vercel setup in README.md.
